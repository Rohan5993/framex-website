FAQ

Questions
founders ask
first.

We get most of our business through referrals. Why change?

Don't replace referrals; extend them. Referrals work because trust transfers. The limit is reach: they can't get you to buyers outside your network, and even referred buyers check you out first. Research found more than half of buyers had ruled out a referred firm before speaking to it, most often because they couldn't tell what the firm did. We make sure a referral lands on positioning that confirms it.

We tried LinkedIn. It didn't work.

Usually one of three things went wrong. The profile didn't say who you're for, the content was about you rather than the buyer's decisions, or the outreach opened with a pitch. Those are positioning and conversation problems, not channel problems. The diagnostic will show which one it was. If LinkedIn genuinely isn't your leak, we'll tell you.

Our founder has no time.

The system is built around that. Expect about four hours in the first 14 days, mostly interviews and approvals. After that, it's a 30-minute monthly interview, a monthly review call, and a few minutes a week approving content. What we can't replace is the founder's judgment. That's the asset, and we need access to it, not hours of it.

How fast will we see something happen?

Setup takes 14 days and execution starts on day 15, so month 1 is a working month. In month 1 we aim for initial traction: target accounts viewing the profile, fit accounts downloading the first resource, and the first trigger-led conversations. Qualified opportunities in a committee-driven sale usually take longer to form, which is why we review every month and recommend at least three months together.

Is this lead generation?

No. Lead-gen agencies optimise for meetings booked. We build the conditions for buyers to understand, trust and choose you, and we start conversations only when there's a real reason to talk. You get qualified opportunities with context, not a calendar full of curiosity calls.

You don't know our technology.

We don't pretend to. We won't impersonate your engineers or your CISO, and we won't publish technical claims you haven't approved. Our expertise is how technology gets bought: the buying committee, evaluation, security review, the business case. You bring the technical truth, and we translate it into what the buyer needs in order to decide.

How do you measure progress without promising meetings?

Leading indicators first: profile views from target accounts, reach among ICP titles, resource downloads by fit accounts, trigger-led conversations started and qualified. Then the lagging indicator: opportunities handed over, and how good they are. We review both with you monthly and change what isn't working.

Why three months?

Month 1 gets the system live and creates initial traction. Month 2 uses that data to sharpen positioning, content and triggers. Month 3 compounds what works. A buying committee that sees your thinking for one month may remember you; one that sees it for a quarter starts to shortlist you.
