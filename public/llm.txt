# FrameX

> FrameX builds the Authority-to-Pipeline System for B2B technology companies — the
> infrastructure for how the market finds, understands, trusts, and buys from a vendor,
> built primarily on LinkedIn. Built in 14 days, live on day 15.

## What FrameX does

FrameX closes the gap between a B2B tech company's delivery quality and its market
position. Most B2B buying committees rank their shortlist, vet vendors, and build trust
before ever speaking to sales — often on LinkedIn. FrameX builds the system that makes
sure a company is found, understood, trusted, and chosen during that invisible phase.

## The six-step Authority-to-Pipeline System

1. **Positioning Architecture** — ICP, buying triggers, message map, founder/team
   profiles. Fixes: being understood in one read.
2. **Authority Content** — editorial architecture, founder posts (8–16/month), monthly
   capture interviews. Fixes: being found and trusted.
3. **Sales Resources** — flagship resource, landing page, champion kit (internal pitch,
   CFO one-pager, security answers). Fixes: trust and buy-in.
4. **Signal Intelligence** — trigger map, account lists, weekly signal log, monthly
   signal review. Fixes: finding the right people at the right time.
5. **Conversation Framework** — conversation playbook, manual outreach handling,
   qualification notes, a shared conversation ledger. Fixes: starting with context
   instead of a pitch.
6. **Pipeline Revenue** — qualified opportunity handovers with problem, trigger,
   stakeholders and notes. The client's own sales team closes.

## Engagement model

- Build: days 1–14 (onboarding, positioning brief, profile rebuild, first content and
  resource — roughly 4 hours of the client's time).
- Live: from day 15 (profile, posts and flagship resource go live; signal monitoring and
  trigger-led conversations begin; roughly 30 minutes a week of the client's time).
- Minimum recommended engagement: three months (month 1 traction, month 2 sharpening,
  month 3 compounding).

## What FrameX is not

Not a lead-generation agency (it does not optimize for meetings booked) and not a
technical consultancy (it does not claim domain expertise in the client's product —
it translates the client's own technical truth into what buyers need to decide).

## Key stats referenced on the site

- 94% of B2B buying groups rank a shortlist before contacting a vendor (6sense); the
  pre-contact favorite wins 77% of the time.
- 71% of "hidden" buyers in a deal have little or no contact with sales (Edelman–LinkedIn).
- Only ~0.3% of B2B tech companies post more than twice a week on LinkedIn.
